# Gölgedeki Miras — Story Bible

## Core premise
A young heir inherits the North Kingdom after a night of battle. Their father survives only as a memory and a mystery: in the opening scene his face remains hidden beneath a battle helm. Before disappearing, he leaves the royal seal and warns the heir that the kingdom's inheritance is a responsibility, not a possession.

The central mystery is tied to the North Gate, an unfinished map and three broken oaths. The player rebuilds the kingdom while discovering why the father's final campaign was erased from the official record.

## Tone and themes
- Medieval political mystery, family legacy and rebuilding after conflict.
- Hopeful but grounded; consequences emerge from decisions, not shock value.
- The people, commanders and council remember the player's choices.
- Food, shelter, safety, loyalty and diplomacy are all part of the inheritance.

## Opening sequence
1. Night alarm at North Castle; fog obscures the battlefield.
2. The father appears as a silhouette in a helm; his face is never shown.
3. He passes the royal seal and warns the heir to protect people and investigate the North Gate.
4. Morning reveals the castle still standing, the council divided and the father's chair empty.
5. The player chooses whether to protect the people first or investigate the North Gate.

## Initial branching choices
- protect-people: villagers and families become the moral center of the opening chapter.
- seek-truth: the North Gate mystery becomes the opening priority.
- repair-walls: defense and commander confidence take priority.
- feed-people: food security and public trust take priority.

Choices are stored as versioned local browser story progress. They are not yet connected to cloud saves or a complete ending system.

## Campaign arc
- Prologue — Kuzey'de Son Gece
- Chapter I — Sessiz Surlar
- Chapter II — Taş ve Ekmek
- Chapter III — Sancağın Altında
- Future chapters — three broken oaths, lost regions, former allies, the truth about the father's last campaign, and the final legacy.

## Narrative implementation status
The current playable build includes the opening story overlay and its first branching choices. Later chapters, full quest integration, voiced dialogue, cinematic character models and final endings remain future production milestones. Do not present them as completed until implemented and tested.
